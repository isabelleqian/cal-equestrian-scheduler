Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

The Cal Equestrian Lesson Scheduler is used by members of the Cal Equestrian Team at UC Berkeley to share weekly availability so club officers can form group lessons.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name and email address. You also give us your driver status and the weekly times you mark as available.

How we use it

Club officers use this information only to schedule lessons and contact members about them. We do not sell or share it with anyone outside the club, and we do not use it for advertising.

Where it is stored

Data is stored in a Supabase database. Availability is cleared at the start of each semester.

Deleting your data

To have your account and data removed, contact the club officers at isabelle.qian@berkeley.edu.
